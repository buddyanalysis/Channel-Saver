Channel Saver — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026 · Developer: Asif (LetRestart) · Contact: facebook.com/masifghafar

Channel Saver is a browser extension for YouTube research. This page explains what data it handles, where that data goes and what control you have.

1. Data stored on your computer only

  • Channels, niches, saved videos, notes, tags, settings, competitor upload history and CTR Tester images are stored in your own browser (chrome.storage.local).
  • Automatic backups are saved as a file in your own Downloads folder (Channel Saver Backups).
  • We do not have a copy of this data and cannot see it.

2. Data sent to our licensing server (letrestart.com)

  • Your activation key, a random install id, the extension version and basic computer details are sent to letrestart.com when you activate, when a new version starts, and at most a few times a day to check that the key is valid.
  • The computer details are: operating system, graphics card name, number of CPU cores, memory size, screen size, language and timezone, plus a one-way hash (fingerprint) of these. They are used only to keep one key on one computer and to show the key owner which computer a key is used on.
  • The server answers with the name the key was issued to and its expiry. Nothing else is sent: no browsing history, no YouTube activity, no saved channels.

3. YouTube

  • Channel Saver reads public YouTube pages (channel pages, video pages, search results) to show statistics. These requests go directly from your browser to YouTube.
  • It never signs in to, changes or posts from your YouTube account. The "Assisted reply" helper only writes a draft into the reply box; you press YouTube's own Reply button yourself.

4. Optional AI features (only if you add your own key)

  • If you add your own API key for Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude or OpenAI in Settings, and click an AI button, the content needed for that request (for example a thumbnail image, video titles or a comment you want a reply draft for) is sent directly from your browser to the provider you chose, under that provider's privacy policy.
  • Your API key is stored only in your browser and is never sent to us or included in backups.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your data with anyone.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • We do not collect your browsing history or personal information.

6. Your control

  • Export or back up your data any time from the dashboard (Tools & settings).
  • Remove the activation key under Settings → Activation. Uninstalling the extension deletes all of its data from your browser.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
