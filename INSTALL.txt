CHANNEL SAVER — Install guide (2 minute)
=========================================

Kuch aur install nahi karna. Na Node.js, na koi program. Sirf Google Chrome chahiye.


STEP 1: ZIP kholein
-------------------
1. channel-saver.zip par right-click karein, phir "Extract All..." dabayein.
2. "Browse..." mein Documents chunein, phir "Extract" dabayein.
3. Documents mein "channel-saver" folder ban jayega.
   Is folder ko delete ya move na karein. Extension isi se chalti hai.


STEP 2: Chrome mein lagayein
----------------------------
1. Chrome ke upar address bar mein likhein:  chrome://extensions  aur Enter dabayein.
2. Upar right side par "Developer mode" on karein.
3. Upar left side par "Load unpacked" dabayein.
4. Documents > channel-saver folder chunein aur "Select Folder" dabayein.
5. Upar puzzle icon par click karein, phir "Channel Saver" ke saath pin dabayein.


STEP 3: Activate karein
-----------------------
1. Toolbar mein Channel Saver ke icon par click karein — dashboard khulega.
2. "Activate Channel Saver" ke khane mein woh key likhein jo aap ko admin ne di hai
   (jaise LR-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX), phir "Activate" dabayein. Internet on hona chahiye.
3. Ek key sirf ek computer par chalti hai. Key ki muddat activate karne ke din se shuru hoti hai.
4. Key ke baghair extension kaam nahi karti. Key na ho, expire ho jaye, ya computer badla ho
   to admin se baat karein.

Ho gaya!


YOUTUBE PAR KYA MILEGA
----------------------
- Subscribe ke saath purple "+ Save" button:
  · Video page par yeh VIDEO ko niche mein save karta hai (menu mein upar "Video | Channel"
    switch se channel bhi save kar sakte hain).
  · Channel page par CHANNEL save karta hai.
  Menu mein sab se upar search box hai: niche ka naam likhein. Naya naam ho to wahin
  "Create niche … and save" aa jata hai, ya neeche "New niche" mein likh kar "＋ Create & save".
- Channel page par "🔍 Similar": wahin YouTube par side panel khulta hai ("Looking for you…")
  aur kam az kam 20 milte-julte channels dikhata hai (In-depth = 40), "+ Save" ke saath.
- Har video ke neeche badge: channel ke subscribers, outlier score (jaise 3.2x = channel ke
  aam views se 3 guna) aur views per hour.
- Home, Search aur Subscriptions par neeche "⚡ Filter" button: loaded videos ko outlier,
  views, chhote channels, upload date, Shorts/Long ke hisaab se filter karein.
  "Load more videos" se aur videos khud load hoti hain.
- Kisi channel ke naam par mouse rakhein: us ke stats, "Latest uploads" aur "Most popular" videos.
- Shorts dekhte waqt kone mein box: kab upload hua, views, channel ki typical views,
  channel ki uploads aur channel ki umar.
- Video ke title ke neeche: exact upload time, aur buttons — Thumbnail download, Frame
  screenshot, Swipe file, Similar videos ("Same topic", "Same title format", "Similar channels").
- 💬 Assisted reply: YouTube Studio ke Comments page (aur apni videos) par har comment ke liye
  aap ke templates se naam ke saath reply likh deta hai. Reply AAP khud dabate hain — extension
  kabhi khud post nahi karti.
- Har video ke badge mein 📸: us video ka Share card.
- Jab extension kuch dhoondh raha ho ya kaam kar raha ho, neeche daayein kone mein
  purple "Loading…" dikhta hai.


AI (OPTIONAL — apni key se)
---------------------------
Dashboard → ⚙ Settings → "AI (optional)" mein apni AI key daalein (Google Gemini ki key
free milti hai: aistudio.google.com/apikey). Phir:
- CTR Tester → "🚀 Improve CTR" → "✨ Review with AI": thumbnail ko dekh kar chehra, text,
  emotion, behtar titles aur 3-5 fixes.
- Video page par "✨ Title ideas": us topic par apni video ke liye 8 title ideas.
- Channel ki details aur niche page par "✨ Analyse with AI": format, kya chal raha hai,
  content gaps aur 10 video ideas.
- Assisted reply mein "✨ AI draft": comment ke hisaab se reply ka draft (post aap khud karte hain).
Key sirf aap ke computer mein rehti hai, backup mein nahi jaati.


DASHBOARD (Chrome mein Channel Saver ke icon par click)
-------------------------------------------------------
- Dashboard khulte hi saare tools cards mein: Niche Finder, My Library, Similar channels,
  Competitors, CTR Tester, Swipe file. Kisi tool mein jaayein to left mein
  "← Back to Dashboard" aur sirf usi tool ke pages. Neeche "⚙ Tools & settings" click
  karne par khulta hai (Backup, Export, Settings wagaira).
- 🧭 NICHE FINDER: "Scan" dabayein — YouTube se seedha har category ki sab se zyada dekhi
  gayi videos (24 ghante / 7 / 30 / 90 din) aur un ke channels check hote hain (1-2 minute):
  · Recently added outlier channels — chhote channel jin ki video subscribers se kai guna chali
  · Niches with high future competition — 2 saal se naye channel jo abhi se chal rahe hain
  · Viral videos on small channels — thumbnails, views, VPH, outlier ×, "Random" button
  · Trending keywords — viral titles mein baar baar aane wale alfaaz
  · Most popular categories — "+ Add category" se apni category bhi
  Har section ke upar 24h / 7 / 30 / 90 din ke tabs; "View all" se poora page.
  Channel ke saath "+ Save" (My Library mein) aur 🔍 (similar channels).
- 📚 My Library: saare saved channels: RPM, growth, outlier videos, score, notes. "Recently added" din ke
  hisaab se.
- Bohat saare channels ek saath: "+ Add channels", har line mein ek link. Wahin naya niche
  bhi bana sakte hain.
- 🔖 Need to look, ⚔ Competitors: card ko pakad kar (drag) left menu mein chhod dein.
  Competitors har 30 minute check hote hain (jab Chrome khula ho): har video ka exact upload
  time, kis din aur kis waqt upload karte hain, agli video kab aayegi, pehle 24 ghante ke
  views, title changes. Nayi upload par notification aati hai.
- 🔍 Similar channels: kisi bhi channel ka link daalein. "⚡ Quick search" (~20 channels, jaldi)
  ya "🔬 In-depth search" (~40 channels, naye competitors bhi). Similarity %, avg views, kitne din
  purana, monthly uploads, last upload; kisi bhi column se sort karein. "+ Save" se seedha save.
- 📌 Swipe file: YouTube se save ki hui videos, video ke hisse (hook waghaira) aur thumbnails,
  notes aur tags ke saath.
- 🎯 CTR Tester: 3 thumbnails (A/B/C) aur titles. Preview (Home, Mobile, Search, Sidebar,
  Large, Dark, Squint, Grayscale), Score (contrast, rang, saaf, chhote size par, competitors se
  alag, time-badge corner), "🚀 Improve CTR" (us thumbnail ke liye fix list, rang ka mashwara,
  title tips, Auto-boost aur "📋 Copy instructions" — ek click mein saari hidayaat copy),
  aur Click test (pick rate %). "▶ Test inside YouTube" bhi.
- Niche kholne par us mein save ki hui videos ("🎬 Saved videos") bhi nazar aati hain.
- ⚔ Competitors: kisi competitor par click karein to upar sirf usi ki uploads; ⓘ = details.
- ⚙ Settings: jo feature nahi chahiye use band kar dein.
- Dark/Light mode: upar ☾ / ☀ button — ek click mein badal jata hai.


AUTO-BACKUP (aap ka data mehfooz)
--------------------------------
- Har 12 ghante saara data khud-ba-khud is folder mein save hota hai:
  Downloads > Channel Saver Backups > channel-saver-backup-latest.json
  (aur har din ki alag file bhi). Dashboard mein neeche "Auto-backup: …" par click karke
  foran backup bhi le sakte hain.
- Agar kabhi data chala jaye (jaise extension Remove ho gayi): dashboard mein
  "⤒ Restore from backup" dabayein aur upar wali "latest" file chunein. Sab wapas aa jayega.


AGAR MASLA HO
-------------
- Dashboard mein upar laal patti "Restart now": us par click karein (update ke baad zaroori).
- Save button nazar nahi aa raha: YouTube page refresh karein (F5).
- "Channel Saver was updated": YouTube page refresh karein.
- Channel ka data purana hai: card par ⟳ dabayein. Waise bhi rozana khud refresh hota hai.
- Naya version aaya: dashboard mein upar "Update available" likha aayega.
  "Download" dabayein, ZIP ko usi channel-saver folder par extract karein (files replace karein),
  phir "Restart" dabayein. Aap ka saara data save rehta hai.
  Extension ko kabhi "Remove" na karein — is se saved channels mit jate hain.
- Apna data mehfooz rakhein: dashboard mein "Backup" dabayein. Is se ek file download hogi.
  Naye computer par "Import backup" se wapas le aayein.


ZAROORI BAATEIN
---------------
- Aap ka saara data sirf aap ke Chrome mein rehta hai. Kisi server par nahi jata.
- Har niche ka RPM aap khud set kar sakte hain (niche ke ⋯ menu mein "RPM & notes").
  Woh us niche ke har channel par nazar aata hai.
- Extension sirf YouTube se public data padhti hai. Aap ka YouTube account use nahi karti.
